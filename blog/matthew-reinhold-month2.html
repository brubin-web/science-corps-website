Month 2: Talks, Conferences, Beautiful Vistas and Somber Reflections

By Matthew Reinhold  ·  2026  ·  Monthly Blog

Month 2 began with a busy week of conferences and talks, all taking place on the nearby island of Mindanao. First up was an invited talk at Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology, followed by visits to two local high schools to give talks to their students. The first was quite well attended, but the second one... wow! There must have been over 500 students there! Afterward we travelled to Davao, where I had the privilege of delivering one of the plenary talks on the opening day of the SPVM Physics Conference. The following day, I was scheduled to serve as a judge for the Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) pitching competition. Somewhere amidst all the excitement, I was also approached by the Thai Delegation and invited to give a talk at Bansomdejchaopraya Rajabhat University (don't ask me how to pronounce that!) in Bangkok this coming January. All in all, it was quite an eventful week!

Not long after returning to Bohol, the world was struck by the tragic loss of the legendary Sir Patrick Stewart. It hit me particularly hard, having grown up with his portrayal of the incredible Captain Jean-Luc Picard. Reflecting on my recent academic adventures in Mindanao, I found myself thinking of the following quote from Captain Picard:

"The search for knowledge is always our primary mission."

This captures the spirit of the entire Mindanao experience rather beautifully. It is one of the guiding principles behind what drives me as a scientist and an educator.

On a more personal note, I have found that knowledge enhances beauty — the wonders of nature become even more incredible when we understand what we're witnessing! Over the past month, we've been fortunate enough to experience some truly spectacular sights, from sunrises atop Mount Sagungan to sunsets overlooking Iligan City. We did our best to capture these moments.

— Matthew Reinhold, Science Corps Fellow at CVIF, Jagna, Philippines (Sept 2026–March 2027)
