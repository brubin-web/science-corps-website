One-Page Protocol: Extract DNA from Bananas

Science Corps Update

Fellow Victor Sojo has created a one-page document to help teachers worldwide extract DNA from fruits, using only household items and reagents.

This is part of Victor's work in the Himalayas with our collaborators at Aavishkaar. He is now testing the protocol at local schools, and producing a video that will demonstrate the procedure.
